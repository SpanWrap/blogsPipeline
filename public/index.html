      LLM

      Pick a provider and paste its API key. Groq is free-tier by default (openai/gpt-oss-20b — Llama 3.3 70B was retired). Keys stay on this server, not in git. Free Groq models often cap at 8,000 tokens per minute (input + reserved output). A long template plus research will 413 — keep templates short, or use a model with a higher TPM limit.

      Markdown templates

      Upload .md templates. Headings become required sections; other prose is writing instructions. The whole template is sent to the LLM with the topic and research sources, so keep it short — section headings and a few sentences of guidance, not a full sample article. Optional placeholders: {{title}}, {{body}}, {{sources}}, etc.
